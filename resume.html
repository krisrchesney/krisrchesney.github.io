KEV Watch

Ranks newly published vulnerabilities by how likely they are to land in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog within 90 days. It scores each CVE from what the publisher released, updates daily, and checks its ranking against EPSS. A gradient boosting model reached a ROC AUC of 0.87 on a held-out test set, and the write-up is plain about its limits: it was not better than EPSS at the very top of the list, and the test set had only 37 positive CVEs.

Park Wait Forecast

Forecasts whether an open theme-park ride's posted wait will be at least 45 minutes one hour from now. It is trained on ride wait snapshots collected every 10 minutes, served through a validated FastAPI endpoint, and monitored for data and performance drift. Tests guard against future information leaking into features, and the build prints a "wait stays the same" baseline that the model has to beat. The pipeline and tests are complete, and real-data results are not published yet.

GitHub Issues Triage

Predicts, at the moment a GitHub issue is opened, whether maintainers will close it within 14 days. It is trained on issues from large Google-backed open-source repositories, served through a validated FastAPI endpoint, and monitored for drift. The build uses a feature allowlist, a time-ordered holdout, and tests that truncate the data to the moment each issue was created. Issue queues are ticket queues, so it answers the question a support team asks at intake: which new tickets will stall?

Boxwatch

Plans where your board games go on the shelf. It keeps a collection, pulls each game's stats from BoardGameGeek, and works out how to fit the boxes onto IKEA Kallax, Eket, and Billy shelving, sorted by the priorities you choose (player count, complexity, category, or any order). Each box is drawn to scale inside its cube, games can sit behind other games, and a checked-out game keeps its cube reserved. The live demo uses a sample shelf of well-known games, and your edits stay in your own browser. The full version runs locally and searches all of BoardGameGeek.
